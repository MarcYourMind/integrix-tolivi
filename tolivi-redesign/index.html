How Tolivi works

The social app that gets you off the app.

Tolivi puts activities first. You meet people through something you already want to do, not through endless introductions or empty feeds.

01 / DISCOVER

Find a plan nearby.

Browse events and activities by what interests you, where you are and when you are free.

02 / JOIN

Go because the activity fits.

Join public activities, keep track of the details and connect around a shared reason to be there.

03 / CREATE

Make the plan yourself.

Set the place, time, guest limit and how costs are handled. Then invite people in.

Why it feels easier

A reason to meet comes before the small talk.

Beach walk. Game night. Art workshop. After-work drink. Networking. Tolivi gives the interaction a context before anyone has to make it one.
