One flow

From listing to last entry.

The core host journey is deliberately simple: sell, admit, reconcile.

01 / SELL

Set the event live.

Create pricing tiers, capacity, promo codes and a public page people can share.

02 / DOOR

Scan as people arrive.

Each ticket carries a QR code. The live guest list updates as the room fills.

03 / PAYOUT

Get paid to your account.

Track sales and payouts, issue refunds and handle chargebacks from the same place.

No forced app download

Your event link is the checkout.

Share it on Instagram, WhatsApp or a poster QR. The guest opens the page, pays by card and receives the ticket by email.
