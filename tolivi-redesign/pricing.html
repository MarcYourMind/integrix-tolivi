Pricing

One percentage. No surprise add-ons.

No setup fee, listing fee or monthly fee on standard. You are charged when you sell a paid ticket, and not before.

Standard
10%

of the ticket price. No monthly fee on top.

Business Premium
5%

Half the standard fee, plus premium analytics, placement and expanded team capacity.

Free events
£0

No ticket price, no ticketing fee. Host tools still work.
